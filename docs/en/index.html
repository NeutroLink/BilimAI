    Accuracy in numbers

    Every metric is measured on a sealed held-out set: 112 real school notebook pages, 516 teacher-annotated mistakes. The model never trains on these pages.

    CER 0.038 per-character reading error Of 100 written letters, we read about 96 correctly.
    95.1 % line detection recall Of 100 written lines, we find 95 — almost nothing is lost.
    48 % end-to-end mistake detection Of 100 mistakes, we find 48 and show them to the teacher. Extra marks are removed by the teacher.
    83 out of 100 words read exactly We read 83 of every 100 words exactly as written.

    What it checks

    The five most common assignment types in Uzbekistan’s schools.

    DictationsDictation and vocabulary dictation. The text is known in advance; every deviation is underlined.
    MathSums, equations, word problems. The answer is checked exactly; the line where the mistake begins is marked.
    TestsCircled letters are compared against the answer key: a tick or a cross next to every question.
    Retelling, bayonThe teacher provides the source text: spelling and punctuation are underlined; the content stays the teacher’s call.
    Open questionsTextbook answers are compared against the teacher’s reference: which facts are named, where a possible mistake lies.

    What we don’t check: drawings, outline maps, technical drawings and diagrams. That is a separate task, and we say so honestly.

    BilimAI does not assign grades and does not decide for the teacher — it shows possible mistakes, and the final decision always stays with the teacher.

    Three principles

    The teacher is always in chargeThe program suggests, the teacher decides. The pupil never sees the result — the teacher chooses what to show the child.
    Data stays in the countryPupils’ work is processed and stored on servers in Uzbekistan and is never passed to third parties.
    VerifiableEvery mark shows what it came from. New versions ship only after being checked on a held-out set of works.
