BilimAI

An AI assistant that reads students' written assignments, checks the work, and drafts grades and feedback — so teachers spend their time teaching, not marking. Runs on a custom, fully local AI. Languages: Russian & Uzbek.

View on GitHub Read the plan

How it works

upload → READ → MARK → TEACHER REVIEW → deliver OCR / LLM grades human approves vision vs. rubric every grade

Why local AI

PrivacyChildren's work never leaves the school's servers. No cloud LLM APIs.
CostFixed GPU cost instead of per-assignment API pricing at scale.
ControlFine-tuned on our curriculum, our rubrics, our languages.

Principles

  • Teacher in the loop, always — the AI drafts, the teacher decides. Every correction becomes training data.
  • Eval-gated releases — no model change ships unless it matches teacher agreement on a frozen golden set.
  • Full audit trail — every grade can be explained after the fact: model, prompt, input, output, final human decision.
